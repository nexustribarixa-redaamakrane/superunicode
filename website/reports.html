SuperUnicode Technical Reports

SUTR series — normative specifications, tracked per release.

Technical Reports formalize each contract of the standard. They play the role of Unicode's UAX/UTR documents, and are identified by the SUTR-n scheme with per-version errata.

ReportStatusScope
SUTR-00.1.0SUCS Core — hierarchy, three-space layout, SCP, Traps, Sentinel
SUTR-10.1.0SUTF — SUCS UTF-8/16/32 framing
SUTR-20.1.0SUCA — SuperUnicode Collation Algorithm (binary default)
SUTR-30.1.0SuperUnicode Storage — OWFS/USFS partition policy
SUTR-40.1.0ExtSUCS Transport — vector, vsutf, esutf
SUTR-50.1.0Plugin Lifecycle & Blob Format — stage, checksum gate, mount, registry
SUTR-60.1.0Unicode Compatibility Bridge — the permanent 1:1 guarantee

Core invariants every report honors

  • SUCS_CP is 31-bit; sucs_ex_char_t is 64-bit.
  • 0x000000–0x10FFFF is permanently 1:1 with Unicode.
  • 0x7FFFFFFF is the Sentinel; no allocation exists at or below it.
  • Plugin codepoints start strictly above 0x7FFFFFFF and mount only after the checksum gate.
