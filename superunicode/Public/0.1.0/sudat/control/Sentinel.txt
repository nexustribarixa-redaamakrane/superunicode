# Sentinel.txt - SUCS_CP_SENTINEL (starter)
VALUE  0x7FFFFFFF
ROLE   End-of-stream marker; not a printable allocation. Stops cursor
       advance and terminates the current machine-instruction stream.
NOTE   No plugin may allocate AT the Sentinel (0x7FFFFFFF) or in the terminal
       16 (0x7FFFFFF0-0x7FFFFFFF). Plugin ranges lie in the Native plane
       (0x120000-0x7FFFFFEF) or strictly ABOVE 0x7FFFFFFF (see the Extended
       tree).
