Workshops (proposed)

A possible future process, not a current schedule.

SuperUnicode does not currently hold workshops. No workshop has been scheduled, and no workshop procedure currently reviews proposals or assigns registry slots or native allocations.

This page describes a possible future process. A workshop process would need to test proposals against the existing invariants — the position-fixed Unicode Bridge, Sentinel protection, and range uniqueness — and would have to document how a proposal becomes normative. The repository’s Public trees include working-draft areas for future releases, but those areas are not a workshop queue and do not imply that a proposal has been reviewed or accepted.

To discuss a proposal today, use the public issue tracker linked from Membership and participation.
